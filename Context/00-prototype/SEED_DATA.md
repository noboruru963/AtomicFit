## 1. Dates are relative to first launch (data persists)
- Data survives restarts (AGENTS.md F-06), so there is **no fixed demo clock**. The app uses `SystemClock`; tests use `FixedClock`.
- On **first launch only**, seed sessions at offsets from the first-launch moment **T**: newest session **T − 13 days**, micro session **T − 16 days**, oldest **T − 30 days**, keeping the clock times 19:35, 20:11 and 13:34. (The PDFs show 1 Nov, 29 Oct and 15 Oct relative to Sat 14 Nov 2026; those dates are illustrations only.)
- Effect: the user starts **inactive (7+ days)**, so the quiz prompt appears (demo script step 3). After the user logs workouts, time moves normally.
- Time zone: the device's IANA zone (via `flutter_timezone`); default `Asia/Jakarta` if unavailable.
- Debug builds only: **Reset demo data** clears all stores and reseeds relative to the moment of the reset.
- Provide a `Clock` interface with `SystemClock` and `FixedClock`.

## 7. Calendar [IP]
The current-month calendar marks days with a completed workout and highlights today. Do not hard-code days (the HTML prototype did; that was a flaw).
