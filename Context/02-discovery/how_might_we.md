# How Might We?
**Presented by:** Jonathan Gouw, Alexander Marchiano, Luiesrelio

## Personas & Insights
*   **High Schooler Newbie:** Needs structure; stops for weeks without guidance.
*   **Fit Undergraduate:** Juggling academics; types to log but typing breaks rhythm.
*   **9-5 Gymbro:** Unpredictable schedule; breaks lead to heavy guilt.
*   **On-the-go Mom:** Wants health but handles 100 things; interruptions make workouts stressful.

**Core Insight:** The pain isn't forgetting numbers. The pain is that a lost count makes the session feel like it "didn't count." Demotivation hits hardest for those whose only reward is visible progress.

## Levers
*   **Mechanical:** Typing effort, forgetting notebooks, lost position mid-set, unpredictable length, no knowledge of form.
*   **Psychological:** "It didn't count" mentality, start/chore/stop/guilt cycle, intimidation, no benchmark.

## The 17 HMW Questions
1. Let someone record a set without stopping, typing, or remembering?
2. Keep the record with the person so nothing gets left at home?
3. Keep someone's exact place when life pulls them away?
4. Make tracking cost less effort than the workout?
5. Get a useful count from someone who never counts live?
6. Make a 15-minute session as valid as a 45-minute one?
7. Make a broken session still feel like it counted?
8. Show "you did more than last time" without math?
9. Turn a long break into a fresh start instead of a failure?
10. Cut the guilt out of the start/chore/stop cycle?
11. Tie every logged rep back to personal reasons for training?
12. Tell a beginner what to do next without a trainer?
13. Build confidence for someone scared of gyms?
14. Restart at the right weights after weeks away?
15. Keep all of this free for notebook users?
16. Adjust today's plan to today's energy without user input?
17. Make working out a relief for a parent?

## 3 Chosen HMWs & Crazy 8's Ideation

### Problem 1: How might we let someone record a set without stopping, typing, or remembering?
1. **Voice Recording**
2. **One-Tap Log**
3. **Auto-Detection (Sensors)**
4. **Smart Watch Tracking**
5. **Camera Recognition (AI estimation)**
6. **Quick Gesture (Shaking/Tapping)**
7. **Preset Sets (Auto-prepare next set)**
8. **Automatic Workout Timeline (Background tracking)**

### Problem 2: How might we turn a long break into a fresh start instead of a failure?
1. **"Welcome Back" Stat Reset Choice**
2. **Auto-Ramp-Down Weights (Smart Ease-In)**
3. **The 3-Minute Re-Ignition Workout**
4. **Seasons Episode Framing (e.g., "Season 2")**
5. **Memory Lane Wins Recap (Highlight reel)**
6. **No-Judgment Diagnostic Quiz (Energy check-in)**
7. **"Pause" History Log (Retroactive rest blocks)**
8. **Fresh Start Theme/Skin Unlock**

### Problem 3: How might we make a broken session still feel like it counted?
1. **"Save Point" Resume Card**
2. **Weekly Rep Bucket Visualizer**
3. **"Micro-Session" Auto-Classifier ("Power Blast")**
4. **Lock Screen Quick-Add Counter**
5. **End-of-Day Rep Collector Notification**
6. **Voice-Drop Quick Log**
7. **Flexi-Goal Adjuster (Scales goal dynamically)**
8. **Asynchronous Set Stitching (Merges sets at midnight)**
