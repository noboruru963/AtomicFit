# Software Requirements Engineering (SRE)
**Presented by:** Jonathan, Luiesrelio, Alexander

## Interview / Survey Demographics (15 Responses)
*   **Age:** Predominantly 18-20 range, with a few outliers (16, 17, 25, 42, 43).
*   **Gender:** 10 Male, 4 Female, 1 Prefer not to say.
*   **Occupations:** Mostly students (Software Engineering, Psychology, etc.), plus Senior Software Engineer, Business Owner, Housewife.
*   **Exercise Frequency:** Bulk are 0-2x a week (10 users). 3-4x a week (4 users). >5x a week (1 user).
*   **Lost Track of Progress?** Yes (12), No (2).

**Reasons for losing track:**
*   Managing family/company business mid-workout.
*   Forgetting to count reps / losing focus.
*   Distracted by music/videos or sudden thoughts.
*   Cutting workouts short due to errands/childcare.

**Would you pay for an automated solution?**
*   Maybe: 10
*   No: 4
*   Yes: 1

---

## 6 Specific User Personas

### Persona 1: Kevin, 20 (University Student)
*   **Goals:** Cost-efficiency, optimized workouts/diets, automated routines.
*   **Frictions:** Manual tracking, subscription paywalls, heavy coursework/internship constraints.

### Persona 2: Joshua, 25 (Graphic Designer)
*   **Goals:** Career/health balance, flexible gym schedules (weekend heavy).
*   **Frictions:** Inefficient workouts, rigid app systems that don't support advanced protocols, shifting availability.

### Persona 3: Cindy, 26 (Part-Time Accountant)
*   **Goals:** Gamified experience (level-ups), Social sharing/milestones.
*   **Frictions:** Overhead of manual tracking/timers, social pressure to maintain unbroken consistency.

### Persona 4: Jamie, 17 (High-School Student)
*   **Goals:** Boost confidence/aesthetics, low-barrier entry, approachable habit-building.
*   **Frictions:** Gym intimidation, zero budget/transportation, busy academic/social life.

### Persona 5: Tony, 50 (Senior Software Engineer)
*   **Goals:** Longevity/health, flexible routine to match unpredictable meetings, balancing family/work.
*   **Frictions:** Constant schedule interruptions, family/business take priority, static programs don't work.

### Persona 6: Sarah, 43 (Housewife)
*   **Goals:** Functional wellness, seamless progress tracking, integration into daily life.
*   **Frictions:** Interruptions cause tracking failure, rigid 45-minute plans fail when she only has 15 minutes, high mental friction to start.

## Target Audience Breakdown
1.  **Students:** Exercise for health/aesthetics; highly budget-restricted.
2.  **Young Working People:** Maximize sessions around routines; highly receptive to digital tools.
3.  **Middle-Aged Working People:** Job/Family first; exercise for longevity.
4.  **Stay-At-Home Parents:** Errands/housework priority; highly fragmented time.

## Redefined Problem Statement
*Can we have easy access to a personal trainer who can reduce the cognitive load of exercise, track progress automatically, and accurately design our fitness program according to our own specific needs?*
