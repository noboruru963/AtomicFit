# Problems Behind Workout Tracking (Persona 2.0)
**Presented by:** Alexander Marchiano, Jonathan Gouw, Luiesrelio
*Date:* 18 September 2026

## Core Interview Structure
1. Motivation & Obstacles.
2. Current tracking methods & reasons for using them.
3. Inconveniences of current solutions.
4. Experiences with losing track of progress.
5. Impact of losing progress on motivation.
6. Ideal solutions.

---

## Raw Interview Data

### The High Schooler Newbie (4 Interviews)
*   **Motivations:** Gain weight, aesthetics, peer confidence, prep for sports.
*   **Obstacles:** Low energy post-school, procrastination loops, intimidated by gyms, no budget.
*   **Tracking:** Relies on memory, cousins, YouTube (no counting).
*   **Wants:** Free "coach in pocket", auto-counts reps, visually shows progress ("you did more than last week"), calorie calculators.

### The Fit Undergraduate (3 Interviews)
*   **Motivations:** Weight management, stress relief, budget constraints.
*   **Obstacles:** Shifting class schedules, rhythm broken by manual typing in Notes apps.
*   **Tracking:** Free notes apps with tally marks, physical notebooks (often forgotten).
*   **Wants:** Flexible/adaptive plans, visual tracking, zero-subscription auto-counters (camera/wearable), simple weekly logs.

### The Corporate Lifter (2 Interviews)
*   **Motivations:** Longevity, energy for family, doctor recommendations.
*   **Obstacles:** Sudden meetings/on-call work cuts sessions short, making them feel unproductive.
*   **Tracking:** Smartwatches (passive metrics like HR, no manual reps), mental notes.
*   **Wants:** Adaptive system that detects pauses, resumes seamlessly, and provides low-effort weekly summaries.

### On-the-Go Mom (3 Interviews)
*   **Motivations:** Functional strength for childcare, claiming time for herself.
*   **Obstacles:** Unpredictable childcare demands, chores, errands. Apps expecting 45 uninterrupted minutes fail her.
*   **Tracking:** Physical planners/notebooks (often rushed and forgets to log).
*   **Wants:** Fully automatic background tracker that handles highly fragmented sessions and remembers exact stoppage points.

### The 9-to-5 Gym Bros (2 Interviews)
*   **Motivations:** Aesthetics, longevity, confidence.
*   **Obstacles:** Internal dislike of the "chore" of lifting, traffic, "start->chore->stop->guilt" cycle.
*   **Tracking:** Free apps (Caliber), Garmin watches. Breaks cause total loss of routine.
*   **Wants:** Highly automated, low-friction dynamic planner adjusting to readiness/energy post-break, peer support features.

## Synthesis

**Universal Problems:**
*   No real/accurate tracking system.
*   Tracking methods constantly break down.
*   Loss of data heavily hurts motivation (Data loss = Motivation loss).

**Common Problems:**
*   Busy Schedules.
*   Losing count due to interruptions.
*   Financial Constraints.
*   Motivation Decay.

**What They Are Looking For:**
*   Automatic Tracking (no manual input).
*   Free / Low cost.
*   Dynamically adapt to schedule.
*   Progress Visualization.

---

## Redefined Problem Statement
*Can we give people affordable, low-effort access to expert-level guidance that automatically tracks their progress, adapts their program to real-life disruptions, and designs a plan suited to their individual needs without requiring the time, money, or specialized knowledge a personal trainer normally demands?*
